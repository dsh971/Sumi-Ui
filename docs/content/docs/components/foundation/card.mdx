## Props

| Prop | Type | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `variant` | `"plain" \| "bordered"` | `"plain"` |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

`plain` sets the content under a single hairline with no fill or box.
`bordered` is a white sheet with a hairline edge for dense app UI. Cards never
carry a shadow; the `default` and `elevated` variants were removed in 3.0.0.

`CardHeader`, `CardBody` and `CardFooter` take their side padding from the variant: flush (`px-0`) in `plain`, inset (`px-5`) in `bordered`. Your own `px-*` on a section overrides it.

`CardHeader`, `CardBody`, and `CardFooter` each accept every native `<div>`
attribute — they're layout-only, no required props.
