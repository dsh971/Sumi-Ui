## Props

`Logo` extends `React.SVGAttributes<SVGElement>`, plus:

| Prop | Type | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `variant` | `"light" \| "dark"` | `"light"` |
| `width` | `number` (height scales proportionally) | — |
| `aria-label` | `string` | — |

`variant` picks the logo's own ink color for the surface it sits on — it
does not follow the page's dark-mode toggle automatically, since a logo is
often placed on a fixed-color brand surface independent of the current
theme.
