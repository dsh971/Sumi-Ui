## Props

`TooltipProvider` and `Tooltip` mirror their Radix UI equivalents exactly —
wrap your app (or at least the section using tooltips) in one
`TooltipProvider`.

`TooltipContent` extends Radix UI's `Tooltip.Content` props, plus:

| Prop | Type | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `side` | `"top" \| "right" \| "bottom" \| "left"` | `"top"` |

Keep tooltip copy brief — 12 words or fewer.
